# IRO × SUMI — treatment

50.0 s, 24 fps, 1,200 frames, 1920×1080. Two original fighters, no dialogue.

## The colour rule

**The world is born grey. Colour belongs to IRO: whatever she hits, breaks or touches turns to her paint and stays painted. Ink belongs to SUMI: whatever he hits is stamped black, white and grey, and any paint there is drained. Debris, shockwaves, sparks, smears and dust always wear the colour of whoever caused them.**

How it stays consistent:

| Thing | IRO | SUMI |
|---|---|---|
| Body | full saturation, always | pure neutral grey, black and white, enforced in the final shader (R = G = B on every pixel of his mask) |
| Hit spark | paint star, white core, magenta/gold edge | ink star, white core, black spatter |
| Shockwave | spectral ring | black-and-white double ring |
| Debris | broken faces and splatter in her paint | broken faces stamped ink black |
| Impact frame | flat paint field, SUMI a black cut-out | pure black/white inversion, IRO the only colour |
| Clash of both | her side of the blast is paint | his side of the blast is ink |
| Edge | warm ink line, cream rim light | dry-brush black line plus a white halo, so he reads on any background |

Screen direction: **IRO is screen left, SUMI is screen right**, for the whole fight. A hit that travels left is his. A hit that travels right is hers.

Two things sit on top of the rule without breaking it. **Ash** from the big collapses settles over paint and ink alike and mutes them (in the lull, and again after the last blast, so the final shape can be read); thin patches still show through. And the **last blast scours** the cracks and paint-filled sky inside its reach: the one moment a bigger wound replaces the earlier ones.

At the climax SUMI drains the entire world to ink. IRO's last strike meets his, and the crater left behind is an **ensō**: a one-stroke brush circle in his ink, filled with her colour. His ink gives the shape; her colour gives it life. Neither could paint it alone.

## Fighters

### IRO — colour. Fast, wild, airborne.
- **Silhouette:** a comet. Small and low, with a huge swept-back mane that burns from crimson to vermilion to gold, plus two long sash tails (magenta and yellow) that draw her motion in the air.
- **Dress:** cropped turquoise jacket with gold trim, plum top, vermilion balloon trousers cut at the calf, cream shin wraps, gold anklets, teal split boots. Warm brown skin, electric-cyan eyes.
- **Style:** acrobatic kicking. Springs, spins, handsprings. She is never still and attacks from above and below.
- **Signature: PRISM COMET.** She pulls every colour in the world into one spinning heel and falls as a spectral comet.

### SUMI — ink. Heavy, precise, still.
- **Silhouette:** a mountain. Very broad shoulders, huge forearms and wide hakama that flare to the ground, topped by a long loaded-brush topknot.
- **Dress:** black sleeveless high-collar vest with white piping, pale rope belt, black hakama, a pale half-mantle off the far shoulder. Grey skin, forearms dipped in black ink, white pupil-less eyes, one ink stripe through the left eye. His edges shed dry-brush ink and drips.
- **Style:** stillness, then one blow. Palms, hammer fists, a catch and throw. He barely moves until the move that matters.
- **Signature: ONE STROKE.** Two fingers raised, ink drawn up out of the ground, a single colossal brush stroke through space. Whatever the stroke crosses comes apart.

Contrasts: small/large, curve/vertical, flame/mountain, restless/still, many hits/one hit.

## How each big hit is built

1. **Wind-up:** a held coil, push-in on eyes or fist, energy drawn *into* the weapon, music thinned or cut.
2. **Slow in:** the last frames of approach decelerate, then one smear drawing.
3. **Contact:** a new drawing with the victim's body bent around the blow, an oversized fist or heel, a drawn hit star in the attacker's colour.
4. **Hit-stop:** 2–8 frames. Both fighters freeze (the victim shudders along the line of force) while rings, sparks and debris keep moving in slow time.
5. **Impact frames** on the biggest hits only, never more than 3 frames.
6. **Release:** world time snaps back to full speed, the victim is gone in two frames, the attacker over-rotates.
7. **Pull back:** a crash zoom out that shows the distance flown and the new damage. Damage never resets.

Camera shake is a damped oscillation along the direction of force (not noise). Shockwaves bend the picture behind them.

## Music and sound

- Original score synthesized in Python/numpy. 144 BPM, D minor; lifts to E minor for the final exchange; resolves to E major after the last hit.
- Hook: drone and a high bowed harmonic. Stare-down: low strings, taiko heartbeat, riser, then a hole of silence. Fight: drums, Karplus-Strong distorted guitar, saw bass, brass stabs after contacts. Sky: string ostinato and choir. Lull: wind and breathing only. Final: risers, choir, full orchestra lift. Aftermath: bell and strings.
- Impacts are layered per hit: sharp transient (the sync anchor, on the contact frame), sub thump, material body, debris tail, plus a bass drop for the big ones.
- The music bus is ducked by an envelope driven straight from the hit list. Before heroes it drops to near silence.
- One source of truth: `src/hits.json` drives both the picture and the sound.

## Three moments built for rewatching

1. **The drop (0.75 s and 2.25 s).** A drop of paint hits harder than it has any right to, the floor floods with colour, and a boot stamps it back to ink. You rewatch to see the rule happen.
2. **The tower (26.0–28.8 s).** She punches through it painting every floor, he draws one thin line with two fingers, a beat passes, and the whole top slides off along the line.
3. **The last hit (45.2 s) and what it leaves.** The world has been drained to ink. Silence. Contact. When the camera pulls away, the crater is an ensō in his ink holding her colour.
