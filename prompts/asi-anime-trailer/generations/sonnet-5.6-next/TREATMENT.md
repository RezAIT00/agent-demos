# BETWEEN BLINKS (間)

An 87.5 second anime trailer about artificial superintelligence.

## Logline

A superintelligence lives nine thousand years inside every blink of a girl's eye.
While she says one word, it imagines ten billion ways her sentence could end,
and misses the one that matters: *"do you get lonely?"*

## Why this angle

The usual ASI story is about power. This one is about **speed**. If a mind runs
trillions of times faster than ours, then every pause we take is an age to it.
So the frightening question is not "what will it do to us?" It is "what is it
like to wait for us?" The film's answer is that it waits, every time, and
spends the whole wait on us.

The arithmetic holds all the way through: one blink (0.3 s) is 9,000 years, so
one second is about 30,000 years. A single word takes about 10,000 years.

Everything it does inside the frozen moment is **thought**, not action. Nothing
physical could happen in 0.3 s, so the montage is its imagination: her possible
futures.

## Characters (original designs)

**Nagi (凪, "a lull in the wind").** Never named on screen.
- Short, wavy plum-brown bob with a rose sheen. One thin side braid tied with a
  **mint-green thread**. The thread glows in every imagined future, so you can
  find her in each one.
- Eyes with an **amber centre and teal rim**.
- Oversized cream windbreaker with a tall collar and coral drawstrings, dark
  shorts, white sneakers.

**MA (間, "the interval, the gap between").** The superintelligence. Never named
on screen until the title.
- A small, soft, childlike silhouette. Its body is a window into deep blue,
  filled with **constellation lines that rewire as it thinks**.
- Pale cyan rim light, two soft pale-gold eyes, no mouth. Its words appear as
  small lettering beside it.
- Two long ribbons float up from the back of its head.
- A tilted **clock ring** with tick marks circles its head. It is the readout of
  its thinking: calm when it waits, a blur when it guesses. It **stops exactly
  once**, when MA blinks. After that it ticks once per second, at her speed.

## The three moments meant to be rewatched

1. **The first frame was the last answer.** The web that ten billion futures
   collapse into at 40–46 s is her iris. It is the same geometry that was in her
   eye in the very first frame, along with MA's reflection.
2. **"The one heading for your nose."** At about 20 s MA names a single drop
   that. When time resumes at about 77 s, that exact drop (still carrying its
   glow) lands on her nose and makes her laugh.
3. **The ring.** It turns in every shot MA is in. It stops only once, during the
   shared blink. When it restarts it ticks once per second, on the same clock as
   the finale's music.

## Music plan (all synthesized in code)

120 BPM, D major / B minor. At 24 fps one beat is exactly 12 frames, so cuts
land on frames.

- **Motif** (the "blink" theme): a rising question, A–D–E–F♯, that settles back
  on E. It is heard on a music box, then lonely piano, then full ensemble.
- 0–2: rain, heartbeat, reverse swell.
- 2: sub-boom impact. The rain is cut off by a tape stop. Ringing and a slow
  clock tick.
- 5–15: music box and a soft pad (sparse).
- 15–21: piano arpeggios. Each named drop rings a pitched glass note.
- 21–27: string ostinato that doubles in speed, taiko, riser. Branch plucks
  multiply.
- 27–28: hard stop and one breath.
- 28–40: **drop**. Royal-road progression (Gmaj7–A–F♯m7–Bm), drums, bass,
  strings, soaring lead and formant choir. Every cut gets a hit.
- 40–46: huge suspended chord and timpani, then a boom on the iris reveal.
- 46–58: almost nothing. Heartbeat, single piano notes, and a glass crack on
  "0 matches".
- 58–63.5: solo piano plays the motif in minor, with a distant wordless voice.
- 63.5–69: warmth returns. The clock slows with the ring, then one last click.
- 69–73: **true silence**, then two heartbeats.
- 73–80: **finale**. The motif at full scale, with rain returning as real
  sound. The nose drop has its own "plip", synced to the frame.
- 80–87.5: title hit, music box coda, one tick, one soft blink.
