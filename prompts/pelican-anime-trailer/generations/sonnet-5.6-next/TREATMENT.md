# BEAK VELOCITY — treatment

*A pelican riding a bicycle. An anime season trailer, played completely straight.*

- **Length:** 72.000 s = 48 bars at 160 BPM (1 beat = 0.375 s = exactly 9 frames at 24 fps).
- **Picture:** 1920×1080, 24 fps. Characters, wheels and crank are drawn on twos (12 drawings/s). Camera, ground, sky and effects run on ones.
- **Tone:** total sincerity. Nobody winks. The comedy comes from how much the film believes in this bird.

## The hero: THE PELICAN
- White body, cel-shaded in three tones with lavender-blue shadows. Warm yellow chest patch.
- Black flight feathers. In the riding pose the black primaries wrap the handlebar horns like fingers.
- Very long orange-gold beak with a red hooked nail, and a hanging throat pouch.
- Long S-curved neck. A crest of swept-back cream feathers reads like anime hair.
- Amber eye on a bare gold skin patch, heavy slanted brow, two white highlights and a star glint.
- **Signature accessory:** a long crimson scarf with two tails and a gold band near each tip.
- **Scar:** two pale slashes across the base of the upper beak.
- Orange legs and big fully webbed feet.

## The bike
- A real diamond frame: seat tube, top tube, down tube, head tube, paired chain stays and seat stays, raked fork. Teal paint, gold collars.
- Two 32-spoke wheels with crossed lacing, chrome rims and tan-wall tyres.
- 45-tooth chainring and 18-tooth cog, so the wheel turns exactly **2.5×** per crank turn. The chain moves at crank angle × ring radius.
- Level platform pedals, cranks 180° apart, cream-taped bullhorn bars, black saddle.
- Leg rig follows real bird anatomy: short feathered thigh to a **forward** knee, shank to a **backward** hock, orange tarsus down to the foot. The forward knee pumps like a cyclist's.
- When coasting, the crank stops and the rear wheel keeps turning (freewheel). In the hairpin the **inside pedal is up**.

## The rival: THE PEREGRINE
A peregrine falcon: slate back, black hood and moustache stripe, barred cream breast, yellow eye ring, violet iris, short hooked beak, swept crest. Rides a matte-black aero bike with a solid disc rear wheel, deep front rim and violet trim.

## Look
Three-tone cel shading with hue-shifted shadows and a hard-edged coloured rim light. Ink lines come from an object-ID and depth pass at 2× resolution; per-vertex weight tapers them to nothing at feather tips, and they are heavier on the underside. Backgrounds are painted in shaders with no ink lines, as in anime. Effects: radial and horizontal speed lines, smear drawings, a few impact frames (never more than one per second), camera shake, crash zooms, Dutch angles, spinning-spoke blur computed analytically.

All lettering comes from two typefaces drawn for this film: a heavy chamfered display face made of polygons, and a single-stroke face for captions. The katakana is drawn the same way.

## Music (original, synthesised in code)
160 BPM, E minor, moving up to **F♯ minor** at the climax.
- 0–3 s: heartbeat, reverse cymbal, steel "shing" as the eye opens, pedal click.
- Lore: one taiko-and-brass hit per card; the failed drawings get a sad, tiny square-wave plink.
- Training: palm-muted chugs and half-time drums; each crash cuts the band dead; rain gets a plucked arpeggio and strings; at GET UP the full band enters and the lead plays the hero theme.
- Rival: tritone low strings, braam brass, taiko.
- Race: drop on GO. Double-time drums, galloping double-tracked distorted guitar (Karplus-Strong), bass, string ostinato, brass stabs.
- Canyon: half-time, choir. Power-up: heartbeat, riser, **dead silence** under the scream, then the hero theme a tone higher with everything playing.
- Title: letter slams, final chord. Stinger: near silence, wet fish, one gulp, one last stab.
- Sound design: freewheel clicks tied to wheel speed, chain whir tied to cadence, wind tied to speed, rain, thunder, skids, sparks, whooshes, impacts, and a synthesised pelican battle cry.

## Three moments built for rewatching
1. **The failed drawings (6.0–8.3 s).** Each file is wrong in a different way and rewards a pause: wheels that miss the frame, a chain wired to the bird, three wheels, feet nowhere near any pedal.
2. **The pouch (30–48 s).** It twitches during the VS split and the race, and a fish tail slips out for a few frames during the neck-and-neck shot. The stinger pays it off: eleven fish, and they can be counted.
3. **The mid-air scream (53.3–57.0 s).** Wings fully spread over a canyon, a bird that could simply fly, and it keeps its feet on the pedals and turns the crank.

## What changed during the build
The plan above was written first. These changes came out of the critique passes:
- The fish shot gained a beat (it now ends at 70.88 s) so the punchline card can be read. It starts tight and pulls back as the pile grows.
- The race shots run on a real 8.5 degree slope with a level horizon. The first version was a flat road.
- The overtake blows past in the foreground, not in the far lane.
- The canyon flight shows both ledges, with the Peregrine stranded on one and looking up.
- A violet moon sits behind the Peregrine at the reveal.
- The scarf beat shows both ends held in the wingtips.
- The title card gained the line NO TRAINING WHEELS.
